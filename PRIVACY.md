# Privacy Policy for Llama Music Player

**Last Updated:** August 4, 2026

---

## Overview

Llama Music Player is a high-quality Android music player built with privacy as a core principle. This document explains our practices regarding data collection and usage.

---

## No Data Collection

Llama Music Player does **not** collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data.

**We do not:**

- Collect your name, email, or any personal information
- Track your location
- Use analytics or crash reporting services
- Collect device information or identifiers
- Access your contacts or call logs
- Use advertising IDs

---

## Local-Only Data

All data processed by Llama Music Player stays on your device. Nothing is ever sent to the internet.

The app stores the following locally:

- **Song metadata** (title, artist, album, genre) — cached in a local SQLite database for faster loading
- **Album art** — cached in memory for display
- **Equalizer settings** — saved to SharedPreferences
- **Playback state** (current song, position, repeat/shuffle mode) — saved to SharedPreferences
- **Selected folders** — saved to SharedPreferences

---

## Permissions

Llama Music Player requests only the permissions necessary for music playback:

- **Storage permissions** — to read your music files
- **Notification permission** — to show playback controls
- **Foreground service** — to play music in the background
- **Bluetooth permission** — to support headset controls
- **Audio settings permission** — to control equalizer and audio effects

All permissions are requested at runtime and are used exclusively for their stated purpose.

---

## Third-Party Services

Llama Music Player uses **no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services**.

The app uses two open-source libraries:

- **jaudiotagger** — for reading and writing ID3 tags
- **FontAwesome** — for vector icons

Both libraries operate locally on your device and do not transmit any data.

---

## Security

All data remains on your device. We do not store, process, or transmit your data anywhere. Your music and preferences stay private and under your control.

---

## Children's Privacy

Llama Music Player does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app has no social features, no user accounts, and no data collection mechanisms.

---

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in the app and this document will be updated with a new "Last Updated" date.
