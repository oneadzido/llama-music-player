# Privacy Policy for Llama Music Player

**Last Updated:** August 19, 2026

---

## Overview

Llama Music Player is a high-quality Android music player built with user privacy as a core principle. This document outlines how the application handles your data.

---

## No Data Collection

Llama Music Player does **not** collect, transmit, or store any personal information.

**Llama does not:**

- Request your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information
- Monitor or track your location
- Incorporate analytics or crash reporting tools
- Gather device identifiers or hardware information
- Access your contacts, messages, or call history
- Integrate with advertising networks or use advertising IDs

---

## Local-Only Storage

All information processed by Llama Music Player remains exclusively on your device. No data is ever transmitted over the internet.

Llama stores the following information locally:

- **Song metadata** (title, artist, album, genre) — cached in a local SQLite database to improve loading performance
- **Album artwork** — cached in memory for efficient display
- **Equalizer preferences** — stored in SharedPreferences
- **Playback state** (current track, position, repeat and shuffle settings) — stored in SharedPreferences
- **Selected music folders** — stored in SharedPreferences

---

## Permissions

Llama Music Player requests only the permissions essential for music playback functionality:

- **Storage access** — required to read your music files
- **Notification access** — required to display playback controls
- **Foreground service** — required to play music while the app is in the background
- **Bluetooth access** — required to support headset and remote controls
- **Audio settings access** — required to control equalizer and audio effects

All permissions are requested at runtime and are used solely for their intended purposes.

---

## Third-Party Services

Llama Music Player integrates **no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services**.

Llama uses two open-source libraries:

- **jaudiotagger** — for reading and writing ID3 metadata tags
- **FontAwesome** — for vector icon rendering

Both libraries operate entirely locally on your device and do not transmit any data.

---

## Security

All data remains securely on your device. Llama does not store, process, or transmit any of your data elsewhere. Your music library and preferences stay private and under your complete control.

---

## Children's Privacy

Llama Music Player does not collect any personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13. Llama contains no social features, user accounts, or mechanisms for data collection.
